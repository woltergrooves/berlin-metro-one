# Playing and controls

Open **https://berlin-metro-one.vercel.app** in Safari or Chrome. On the start screen, choose **New save** (or an existing save), then tap **Start**.

## iPhone and iPad

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Walk | left thumb on the screen (virtual stick) |
| Look around | drag with your right thumb |
| Phone | 📱 button, bottom right |
| Action (pick up, buy …) | the button that appears on screen |

Hold your device in landscape for the best view. Use *Share → Add to Home Screen* to get the game as an app on your home screen.

## Mac (keyboard and mouse)

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| **W A S D** or **arrow keys** | walk (diagonals work too) |
| **Click the right half** | lock the mouse to look around. **Esc** releases it |
| **E**, **Enter** or **Space** | the action button |
| **P** | open and close the phone |

- Dragging with the mouse works too: drag left to walk, drag right to look.
- In Street View, the arrow keys use Google's own controls.
- Safari may ask the first time whether the page may lock the mouse pointer.
- To see the iPhone layout on a Mac, open the game in Chrome, press ⌘⌥I and then ⌘⇧M, and pick an iPhone or iPad.

## Getting around

- **Boarding:** walk through an open door into the train. You get off the same way.
- **Departure boards** on the platform show the line, direction and waiting time.
- **Transfers:** follow the signs in the line's colour to the transfer stairs at the end of the platform. At stations with several lines (Zoo, Alexanderplatz, Nollendorfplatz), a window lets you choose where to go.
- **Planning a route:** open the BVG app on the phone and pick a stop. The top of the screen then shows which line to take, how many stops it is, and when to get off or change.
- **Going outside:** follow AUSGANG to the exit stairs. You come out in Street View at the real station, with a map of the area (Spätis, cafés, stamps, Pfand). The **← U-Bahn** / **← S-Bahn** button takes you back.

## Saves

The game saves automatically in your browser. A save on your iPad is therefore not on your Mac, and the other way round. If you clear your browser's website data, you lose your save.

## GOD menu

The GOD button is meant for testing. It lets you jump to any station on any line (search works across all lines), and it shows tools and the counter for paid Street View loads.
