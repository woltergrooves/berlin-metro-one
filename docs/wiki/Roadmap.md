# Roadmap

The full roadmap (Roadmap 3.0) is in [`docs/ROADMAP.md`](https://github.com/woltergrooves/berlin-metro-one/blob/fase-1/docs/ROADMAP.md).

**The big picture:** first we build a large, authentic Berlin to explore. Then we add life to the city: an economy, characters, jobs, stories and choices with consequences.

## Done

- ✅ Technical foundation: one game state, one save format, one clock, one wallet
- ✅ Graphics upgrade: lighting, materials and real OSM surroundings at above-ground stations
- ✅ All U-Bahn lines U1–U9
- ✅ S-Bahn Stadtbahn and Ringbahn
- ✅ Smartphone-style phone, with a BVG app and on-screen route guidance
- ✅ Mac controls (keyboard and mouse)

## Work plan (in order)

1. **North–South tunnel S1/S2/S25**, with the ghost stations
2. **Tram M10 and Bus 100**
3. **Street View 2.0:** places as pins in the real street view, a destination with compass and mini-map, actions when you get close, and missions on the street
4. **Audio:** "Zurückbleiben bitte", station announcements, running sounds
5. **Small jobs in between:** the trip progress bar, station designs for Ostkreuz and Südkreuz, large-profile trains for U5–U9, updating old tests
6. **Berlin Discovery:** discoveries and stories at the stations, followed by the living city and careers (courier, Späti, bouncer …)
